# Cast redesign — October 2, 2026

The user's reference images guide the design: a bright leafy desk, tabby companion, small floating sidebar, mint composer, mountain-city community cover, and floating atlas/satellite and historical map controls.

The connected application and browser-local preview share components. Preview edits are real; the app never invents an AI reply, another person's activity, or an executed schedule.

- Chat: customizable image/video background and companion, files/plugins library, drag agents into a project, up to ten responders with blue selection rings, and speech when a validated provider is enabled.
- Agent studio: identity, expertise, personality, beliefs, origin, emotional guidance, response style, permitted files, tools, behavior examples, workflows, archiving, restoration, and deletion. Examples guide prompts; they do not train model weights.
- Workflows: editable templates/custom steps; apply-to-all preserves each character. Text templates guide connected AI; image/video execution requires a provider and remains disabled.
- Community: personal feed and networks, accepted memberships/connections, moderated roles, real posts/replies/bookmarks/events/resources, opt-in interest matching, reorderable/removable panels, and an interactive relationship graph. Another person's participation requires connected accounts.
- Map: actual Natural Earth geography, dated NASA satellite composite, styles, zoom/pan/search, private saved places, notes, source links, directions, and curated historical events filtered by date. Modern reference borders are identified in history. Historical military/political and current live layers require sourced providers.

Custom backgrounds stay on the current device. Optional cat visits respect reduced-motion settings. Accurate capability labels and durable saves take precedence over making an unavailable integration appear operational.
